# Brand Identity

## 🎯 Brand Philosophy

Kyron is **the user-owned social stack** — a platform that gives creators and users control over their identity, data, and audience. Our brand reflects this mission through a **clean, modern, and trustworthy** identity.

### Core Values

1. **User Ownership**: You control your data, your identity, your audience
2. **Portability**: Take your social graph with you
3. **Transparency**: Open source, open standards, open governance
4. **Innovation**: AR, vector ranking, and cutting-edge technology
5. **Inclusivity**: Everyone deserves a voice and fair compensation

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Empowering** | Gives users control and ownership |
| **Innovative** | Cutting-edge technology and features |
| **Trustworthy** | Secure, private, and reliable |
| **Inclusive** | Welcoming to all creators and users |
| **Bold** | Not afraid to challenge the status quo |

## 📱 Iconography

### Icon System: Iconsax

Kyron uses **Iconsax Flutter ^1.0.1** for all icons:
- **1,025 glyphs** across all categories
- **2 weights**: Bold and Linear
- **Consistent**: Same icon system throughout

**Icon Usage:**
- **Active State**: Bold weight (`Iconsax.x`)
- **Inactive State**: Linear weight (`Iconsax.x_copy`)
- **Size**: Typically 20px or 24px
- **Color**: Inherits from text or custom color

**Why Iconsax?**
1. **Complete**: 1,025 icons cover all needs
2. **Consistent**: Designed as a cohesive system
3. **Modern**: Clean, simple, recognizable
4. **Flexible**: Multiple weights for different states
5. **Open**: Free to use, MIT licensed

### Icon Guidelines

✅ **Do:**
- Use Iconsax for all new icons
- Use bold weight for active states
- Use linear weight for inactive states
- Size icons consistently (20px or 24px)
- Use `currentColor` for SVG icons

❌ **Don't:**
- Use Material Icons (deprecated)
- Mix icon systems
- Use custom SVG icons (unless necessary)
- Hard-code icon colors
- Use inconsistent sizes

## 🎭 Tone of Voice

### Writing Style

| Aspect | Style |
|--------|-------|
| **Tone** | Confident, friendly, professional |
| **Language** | Clear, concise, jargon-free |
| **Voice** | Active, direct, empowering |
| **Length** | Short, scannable, to the point |

### Messaging Examples

**Value Proposition:**
> "Own your social graph. Keep your audience. Earn your fair share."

**Tagline:**
> "The user-owned social stack"

**Elevator Pitch:**
> "Kyron is a social platform where you own your identity, your data, and your audience. With TikTok-grade discovery, Instagram AR, and YouTube shelf-life, Kyron gives creators the tools they need to build their audience and earn fair compensation — without platform gatekeepers taking 70% of the revenue."

### Content Guidelines

**Headlines:**
- Clear and benefit-focused
- 6-12 words maximum
- Use active voice
- Highlight key value

**Body Copy:**
- Concise and scannable
- Bullet points for features
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Avoid jargon

**CTAs:**
- Action-oriented
- Clear and specific
- Urgency when appropriate
- Examples: "Get Started", "Join Now", "Learn More", "Try for Free"

### Grammar & Mechanics

**Capitalization:**
- Headlines: Title Case or Sentence case
- Buttons: Title Case
- Body: Sentence case

**Punctuation:**
- Use Oxford comma
- Exclamation points sparingly
- No periods in headlines

**Spelling:**
- US English
- Consistent terminology

## 📖 Brand Story

### The Problem

Social media today is controlled by **five gatekeepers** that:
- Own your followers and audience
- Control what you see and when
- Take 70%+ of creator revenue
- Can deplatform you at any time
- Sell your data without consent

### The Solution

Kyron **returns ownership to users** by:
- **Portable Identity**: Your DID is your passport
- **User-Owned Data**: Your content, your control
- **Portable Audience**: Take your followers with you
- **Fair Compensation**: Keep what you earn
- **Open Standards**: AT Protocol for federation

### The Vision

A world where:
- Creators keep 100% of their revenue
- Users control their data and privacy
- Platforms compete on features, not lock-in
- The social graph is portable and open
- Innovation is driven by user needs, not ad algorithms

## 📚 Brand Guidelines Summary

### Quick Reference

| Element | Primary | Secondary | Usage |
|---------|---------|----------|-------|
| **Color** | `#006AFF` | White/Black | Brand identity |
| **Font** | Inter | - | All text |
| **Icons** | Iconsax | - | All icons |
| **Shape** | Pill | Rounded (12px) | Buttons, inputs |
| **Spacing** | 2-40px scale | - | All layouts |

### Do's and Don'ts

✅ **Do:**
- Use the primary logo for all brand representations
- Use `#006AFF` as the primary brand color
- Use Inter for all typography
- Use Iconsax for all icons
- Use pill-shaped buttons
- Use flat interface with hairline separation
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Write in a clear, concise style

❌ **Don't:**
- Distort or stretch the logo
- Use the logo on busy backgrounds without clear space
- Use other color systems
- Use other icon systems (except Iconsax)
- Use Material Icons
- Use shadows for separation
- Use inconsistent spacing
- Use jargon or complex language
